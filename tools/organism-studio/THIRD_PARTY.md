# Asset distribution boundary

All vendor bundles, raw structures and generated engine outputs described below are local-only and excluded from this code candidate. Their hashes in ASSET_MANIFEST.json identify required inputs without redistributing them.

# Sources and third-party material

Three.js and OrbitControls retain vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt (MIT). Exact bundled bytes are recorded in CANDIDATE_MANIFEST.json. 3Dmol retains vendor/3DMOL-LICENSE.txt, its companion notice and the license filename referenced by its bundle (BSD-3-Clause and incorporated-component notices). The original 3Dmol release tag was not recorded; the hash is known. The minified bundle's complete dependency-notice coverage still needs verification before release.

PDB archive data are offered under CC0: https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies . Structure JSON preserves accession, source URL, checksum, chains and construct caveats; original PDB/CIF citations are retained. This policy does not extend automatically to external integrated resources, manuscript text or generated engine outputs.

The saved community fields and derived Organism Orient/MemOrient outputs are local project outputs proposed for inclusion. They require the owner's explicit release decision. These are not validated native placements or empirical observations. Article references in sources.json are links, not copied articles. Generated illustrations and historical private records are excluded.
