This is one generic human model. Healthy/disease presets are illustrative and do not diagnose disease or animate tissue destruction. Anatomical geometry has no measured physical scale and awaits expert review. Clinical probing, histological attachment, gingival margin and bone crest are different landmarks.

Evidence uses source-scoped observations across attached biofilm. The saved density fields use their original assumed grid and arbitrary units. No field is projected into anatomical tissue, and no clinical or molecular contact is inferred. The original studies have unresolved numerical and biological limitations. This viewer performs no growth, receptor binding, immune prediction or protection calculation.
